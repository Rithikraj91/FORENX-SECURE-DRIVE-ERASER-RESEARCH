# NIST SP 800-88 Rev. 2

NIST SP 800-88 Rev. 2 is the current NIST guidance for media sanitization and was finalized on September 26, 2025.

## Definition

Media sanitization renders access to target data on media infeasible for a given level of effort.

## Concepts

### Clear
Logical sanitization while generally allowing continued use of the media.

### Purge
A stronger sanitization approach intended to make recovery infeasible while generally preserving the media.

### Destroy
Physical destruction of the media.

## Rev. 2 Design Implication

Rev. 2 emphasizes an organizational sanitization program, assurance/validation, and use of applicable current standards and approved techniques rather than prescribing one universal overwrite count.

## ForenX

The project should use the wording:

> ForenX is designed/aligned with NIST SP 800-88 Rev. 2 and applicable sanitization standards.

Do not claim NIST certification without formal evidence.
